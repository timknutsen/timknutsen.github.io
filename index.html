Research

Salmonid breeding genomics

Genetic analysis of traits relevant for aquaculture production, including growth, health, disease resistance, and robustness. The emphasis is on models and markers that can withstand the practical demands of selective breeding.

Meiotic recombination

Work on crossover rate and distribution in Atlantic salmon and rainbow trout, with particular attention to sex-specific recombination landscapes and their consequences for genetic change.

Genome resources and data systems

Development and use of genomic resources, including marker panels and reference data, together with data handling systems that make analyses reproducible enough for applied breeding work.

About

Tim Martin Knutsen is a senior scientist at AquaGen AS in Trondheim, Norway. He has a background in biology with specialization in molecular genomics and evolution, and works on genetic analyses intended to improve important traits in salmon aquaculture. His role combines quantitative genetics, molecular data, software-oriented data handling, and scientific reporting.

This site is intentionally compact: research themes, selected outputs, and a few durable links. More complete publication lists are best maintained through journal indexes and Google Scholar.
