Senior Researcher at AquaGen, Ås. I work on the genetics behind salmon and trout breeding, and I build AI tools for breeding data.

About

Most of my work is genomic prediction, GWAS and the data flows that feed them. I lead genetic analysis projects for farmed salmon and trout, and I build the systems that handle the data.

I came to genetics from the laboratory. After five years as a bioengineer I went back to NMBU for an MSc in biology and a PhD in statistical genomics, where I mapped the genes behind milk fat composition in Norwegian Red cattle in collaboration with Geno. I joined AquaGen in 2017.

Over the last few years AI has changed how I work. I use coding agents every day, I help colleagues get started with AI in their own work, and I now build and host the small applications that used to stop at an idea.

Research

  • Genomic prediction

    Genomic breeding values for production and disease traits in Atlantic salmon and rainbow trout.

  • GWAS and genetic architecture

    Mapping loci behind traits in breeding populations, from milk fat in cattle to disease response in salmon.

  • Recombination

    How crossover rate and placement vary between individuals and with phenotypic sex in salmon and trout.

  • Genomic resources

    Genome assembly and population genomics for lumpfish, and deep learning models of regulatory variants in farmed animals.

  • Phenotypes from images

    Computer vision for measuring traits from images, so that more animals can be recorded at lower cost.

  • AI in research

    Coding agents and language models used as research tools. Domain knowledge still decides whether the output is useful.

Code

Recent public projects from GitHub. Some are research pipelines, some are small apps I use myself.

  • wgs-align-varcall Slurm and GATK pipelines for whole-genome alignment and short-variant calling. Base quality recalibration only runs once the known-sites file has been validated, and one site config makes it portable across HPC clusters.
  • beagle_impute Chromosome-wise genotype imputation with Beagle or AlphaImpute2, run through Snakemake.
  • soppkart An interactive map of likely mushroom habitat around Ås, built from public map layers. It logs my own finds and empty searches, so the model improves as I use it.
  • treningsapp A training-plan web app for the phone. Strength on Saturday, running on Sunday, and missed sessions move automatically.
